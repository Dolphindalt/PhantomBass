## 0.1.0

First release.

- `pbe-dsp`: crossover, decimated full-wave integrator, bandpass and gain in Q2.30 fixed point.
- `pbe-plugin`: VST3 and CLAP plugin for band-limited speakers with Gain, Speaker Cutoff and Harmonics knobs, for Linux (x86_64, aarch64) and Windows (x86_64).
